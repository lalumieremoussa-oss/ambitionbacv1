WHAT FOOD DO YOU LIKE?
I. Talking about food and drinks

In English, food and drinks are part of daily life. We talk about what we eat, what we drink, what we like and what we do not like. This lesson helps beginners to speak about meals, food, drinks and preferences using the present simple.

Important: The present simple is used to talk about habits and daily activities, especially eating and drinking.

V. Useful Sentences and Expressions
  • I eat rice and fish for lunch.
  • She drinks coffee every morning.
  • They eat chicken and bread for dinner.
  • Do you eat eggs for breakfast?
  • I am hungry.
  • I am thirsty.
  • What food do you like?
  • It is delicious!
  • This food is too spicy.
VI. Reading – Food and Daily Meals (60 sentences)

Food is very important in everyday life. People eat different meals during the day. Breakfast is the first meal of the day. Many people eat bread in the morning. Some people eat eggs for breakfast. Others prefer fruits and juice. Lunch is usually eaten in the afternoon. Rice is a common lunch meal. Fish and chicken are popular foods. Vegetables are important for good health. Tomatoes, onions, and carrots are often used. Some people eat lunch at home. Others eat at school or at work. Dinner is eaten in the evening. Families often eat dinner together. Soup is common in the evening. Bread is eaten with many meals. Water is the most important drink. People drink water every day. Some people like tea in the morning. Others drink coffee. Soft drinks are popular with young people. Fruit juice is healthy. Orange juice is common at breakfast. Mango and banana are popular fruits. People like sweet food. Cake and chocolate are desserts. Some food is spicy. Pepper is used in cooking. Too much spicy food is not good. People have different tastes. Some like meat. Others prefer vegetables. Children like sweets. Adults eat more balanced food. Food gives energy. Healthy food helps the body. People eat every day. Meals are part of culture. Food brings people together. Eating together is important. Good food makes people happy.

VII. Exercises

Exercise 1: Translate into French

  1. I like rice and beans.
  2. She eats yam and cassava.
  3. We drink water and juice.
  4. They eat vegetables every day.
  5. He drinks coffee in the morning.

Exercise 2: Translate into English

Chaque matin, la famille prend le petit-déjeuner ensemble. Ils mangent du pain avec du beurre et du miel. Le père boit du café et la mère boit du thé. À midi, ils mangent du riz et du poisson. Le soir, ils mangent des légumes et du poulet.

Exercise 3: Answer the questions

  1. What food do you like?
  2. Do you like rice or bread?
  3. What do people eat for breakfast?
  4. Do they drink water every day?
  5. What drinks do people like?

Exercise 4: Complete with eat / drink / like

  1. They ______ rice for lunch.
  2. She ______ tea every morning.
  3. Children ______ sweets.
  4. People ______ water every day.
  5. Families ______ dinner together.
Conclusion

Talking about food helps beginners communicate easily in English. Vocabulary about meals, food and drinks is essential in daily life. With regular practice, learners can express their preferences clearly.
