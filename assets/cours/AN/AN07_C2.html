WHAT TIME IT IS?
I. Donner l’heure en anglais

Pour dire l’heure, on indique généralement les minutes en premier puis les heures. Voici quelques exemples :

AnglaisFrançais
It’s ten past eightIl est 8h10
It is forty past sixIl est 6h40
It’s ten to sevenIl est 6h50
It is five to fourIl est 3h55
Remarques :
  • O’CLOCK = pile → It’s 8 o’clock = Il est 08:00
  • QUARTER = quart d’heure → It’s a quarter past eight = Il est 8h15
  • HALF = demi-heure → It’s half past eight = Il est 8h30
  • MIDDAY = NOON = midi → It’s noon = Il est midi
  • MIDNIGHT = minuit → It’s midnight = Il est minuit
  • On utilise am (matin) et pm (après-midi / soir) → 5 am = 5h du matin, 5 pm = 17h

V. Reading : What time is it?

Hello! My name is Sarah. I wake up at seven o’clock every morning. I have breakfast at half past seven. Then I go to school at eight o’clock. My classes start at quarter past eight. I have lunch at twelve o’clock. In the afternoon, I study or play with my friends. I go home at half past three. In the evening, I do my homework at five o’clock. I eat dinner at seven o’clock. At night, I go to bed at ten o’clock. On Sundays, I wake up at nine o’clock and rest. I like to check the time on my clock every hour. It is fun to say the time in English!
