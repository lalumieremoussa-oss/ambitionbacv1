AT THE SPORTS FIELD
I. Talking about sports and activities

Sports are an important part of daily life and teamwork. This lesson helps beginners talk about sports, games, players, teams, and activities on the sports field. You will also learn how to describe matches, training sessions, and the actions of players and supporters.

Important: Use verbs like to play, to run, to win, to lose, to train, to shout, to whistle to talk about actions on the sports field.

III. Useful Expressions
  • Let’s play football! → Jouons au football !
  • Who is the winner? → Qui est le gagnant ?
  • The referee whistles. → L’arbitre siffle.
  • Our team is very strong. → Notre équipe est très forte.
  • We train every afternoon. → Nous nous entraînons chaque après-midi.
  • The supporters shout a lot. → Les supporters crient beaucoup.
IV. Reading – Sports Field Dialogue (60+ sentences)

A: Hello! Are you ready for the match today?
B: Yes, I am excited. Our team trained very well yesterday. A: Did the coach give new instructions? B: Yes, he told the players to focus on passing the ball quickly. A: Great! Who will be the goalkeeper? B: Our best player, John, will be in goal. A: And the forwards? B: Sarah and Tom are our main forwards. They can score many goals. A: I hope we win today. B: We will! Our team is very strong. A: Look! The supporters are already at the stadium. B: They are wearing the team uniform and waving flags. A: The referee whistles! The match starts now. B: Quick, pass the ball to Tom! A: I run fast and pass the ball. B: Good! The player shoots and scores the first goal. A: The supporters shout loudly! B: What a wonderful goal! A: The opposing team is strong. B: Yes, they try to win, but we defend very well. A: The coach whistles. What should we do? B: Focus on teamwork and pass the ball to the center. A: I see. I pass the ball carefully. B: Excellent! Sarah runs and scores another goal. A: Half-time! What is the score? B: Two goals for our team, one goal for theirs. A: Let’s train harder for the second half. B: Yes, we need to keep running and support each other. A: Second half starts. The referee whistles again. B: Quick, run to your positions. A: I see the ball near our goal. I kick it away. B: Good defense! The supporters cheer for us. A: The opposing team attacks fast. B: Stay calm, defend, and wait for the chance to counter-attack. A: Our striker scores! The score is now three to one. B: Amazing! We are winning! A: The referee whistles again. Is it the end of the match? B: Not yet. There are five minutes left. A: I run to help the midfielder. B: The coach shouts instructions from the side. A: I pass the ball to Sarah. B: She shoots… and scores! Four goals! A: The supporters scream and jump with joy. B: What a victory! Our team is very happy. A: The referee whistles one last time. Match finished! B: We won four to one. Fantastic! A: Let’s shake hands with the opposing team. B: Yes, and thank the referee for the match. A: After the match, we train a little more. B: Yes, we need to improve our passing skills. A: The coach teaches us new techniques. B: The players listen carefully. A: Tomorrow, we have another match. B: I am ready to play and run fast again. A: The supporters will come to cheer us. B: Their encouragement helps us win. A: Sports are important for health. B: Yes, running, playing, and training improve our strength. A: We also learn teamwork and communication. B: Exactly! A: Let’s go home now. B: We will come back tomorrow for training. A: I am tired but happy. B: Me too! What a great day at the sports field!

V. Exercises

Exercise 1: Translate into English

  1. Nous jouons au football au terrain de sport.
  2. L’entraîneur parle avec les joueurs.
  3. L’arbitre siffle le début du match.
  4. Notre équipe gagne le match.
  5. Les supporters sont très contents.

Exercise 2: Complete the sentences with ball / referee / team / goal / coach

  1. The ______ is very strong.
  2. The ______ whistles.
  3. We play with a ______.
  4. The ______ speaks to the players.
  5. Our striker scores a ______.

Exercise 3: Role Play

In pairs, invent a short dialogue: A: A player speaking to the coach. B: A supporter cheering the team. Use the vocabulary and expressions learned.

Conclusion

Talking about sports and the sports field helps beginners communicate effectively in English. Using vocabulary for players, teams, matches, and actions makes conversations realistic. Practicing dialogues, role plays, and exercises improves fluency and confidence.
