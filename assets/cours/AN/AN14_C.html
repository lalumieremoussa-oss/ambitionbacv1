AT THE STATION – TRAVELLING
I. Talking about travelling

Travelling is a common activity in everyday life. At the station or airport, we buy tickets, check schedules, and wait for our transport. This lesson helps beginners communicate about travel, tickets, schedules, luggage, and basic actions using simple English.

Important: Use simple present verbs like to wait, to buy, to travel, to arrive, to leave to describe travel actions.

III. Useful Expressions – At the Station
  • Where is the ticket office? → Où est le guichet ?
  • I would like a ticket to Abidjan, please. → Je voudrais un billet pour Abidjan, s’il vous plaît.
  • What time does the train leave? → À quelle heure part le train ?
  • The train is on platform 3. → Le train est au quai 3.
  • Is the train on time? → Le train est-il à l’heure ?
  • The train is late. → Le train a du retard.

V. Useful Expressions – Travelling
  • I like travelling. → J’aime voyager.
  • We travel by plane. → Nous voyageons en avion.
  • I book a room in a hotel. → Je réserve une chambre à l’hôtel.
  • Do you travel abroad? → Voyagez-vous à l’étranger ?
  • Our holiday is wonderful. → Nos vacances sont merveilleuses.
VI. Reading – At the Station and Travelling (60+ sentences)

This morning, I am at the bus station. I buy my ticket at the ticket office. I check the schedule for the bus to Abidjan. Passengers are waiting patiently. Some have big luggage. The bus arrives on time. People leave quickly. I take my bag and go to the platform. I wait for the bus with many passengers. Children are excited. Some people ask the driver questions. The ticket office is busy. The bus driver checks the tickets. Passengers board the bus slowly. Some people sit near the window. I sit near the door. The bus starts moving. I look at the map. We travel through towns and villages. The scenery is beautiful. The bus stops at several stations. People leave and new passengers board. I check my luggage. I ask about the next stop. The journey is long but comfortable. People talk quietly. Some read newspapers. Some listen to music. The bus moves steadily. We pass schools and markets. The driver follows the schedule. The road is busy. We see buses coming from other towns. Some buses are late. Traffic is heavy at the city center. We arrive at the station in Abidjan. Passengers get off quickly. I find a taxi to go to my hotel. Travelling is always interesting. I enjoy discovering new places. I meet new people. I take photos of landmarks. I ask locals for directions. I check into my hotel. I unpack my luggage. The receptionist welcomes me. I rest for a while. Later, I visit nearby markets. I buy souvenirs. I travel by bus, plane, or taxi. Travelling abroad is exciting. Every trip is a new adventure. I learn new words in English. I practise speaking with passengers. I enjoy my holidays. Planning a journey is important. Checking tickets and schedules helps a lot. Travelling teaches patience and observation. I always remember my trips. The bus journey ends safely.

Conclusion

Talking about the station and travelling helps beginners communicate effectively in English. Vocabulary about tickets, schedules, luggage, and transport is essential for daily travel. With regular practice, learners can express themselves clearly and understand travel-related conversations.
