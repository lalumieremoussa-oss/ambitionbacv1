GETTING AROUND THE TOWN – ASKING FOR DIRECTIONS
I. Talking about directions

In everyday life, asking for and giving directions is very useful. This lesson helps beginners learn how to navigate streets, ask questions, and understand instructions in English. We focus on vocabulary for locations, streets, buildings, and common expressions for asking and giving directions.

Important: Use simple verbs like to go, to walk, to turn, to cross, to ask, to show to describe directions.

III. Useful Expressions – Asking for Directions
  • Excuse me, please. → Excusez-moi, s’il vous plaît.
  • Can you help me, please? → Pouvez-vous m’aider, s’il vous plaît ?
  • I am lost. → Je suis perdu.
  • How can I get to the hospital? → Comment puis-je aller à l’hôpital ?
  • Is it far from here? → Est-ce loin d’ici ?
  • Can you show me… ? → Peux-tu me montrer… ?
  • I would like to know where… is. → J’aimerais savoir où se situe…
  • Take this road straight ahead. → Prenez cette voie tout droit.
  • Turn left. → Tournez à gauche.
  • At the crossroads. → Arrivé au carrefour.
  • At the second traffic light. → Au deuxième feu tricolore.
  • There are buildings in front. → Il y a des immeubles en face.
IV. Reading – Dialogue in the Town (60+ sentences)

A: Excuse me, sir. Can you help me, please? I am looking for the post office.
B: Of course. The post office is not far from here. Go straight ahead on this road.
A: Straight ahead… okay. Should I turn somewhere?
B: Yes. When you arrive at the crossroads, turn left. The post office is just behind the big supermarket.
A: Thank you. Is it near the bank?
B: Exactly. The bank is in front of the post office, and the police station is next to it.
A: Oh, I see. Are there any traffic lights?
B: Yes, at the second traffic light, you turn right. You will see some tall buildings in front. The post office is a little further, on the right.
A: Perfect. Thank you very much for your help.
B: You’re welcome. Have a good day!

A: Excuse me, madam. Can you tell me where the hospital is?
B: Certainly. Walk straight ahead, cross the bridge, and then turn left.
A: Cross the bridge and turn left… okay. Is it far?
B: No, it is near the church. You cannot miss it.
A: Thank you. Are there any shops on the way?
B: Yes, you will see a supermarket and a market on the right side of the road.
A: Great. I also need to find the police station.
B: Walk to the post office, the police station is behind it.
A: Okay, behind the post office. How long will it take to walk there?
B: About ten minutes if you walk carefully.
A: Thank you for your directions.
B: My pleasure. Do you need help with anything else?
A: Yes, how do I get to the bus station?
B: Take the road straight ahead, then turn right at the square.
A: Right at the square… I see. And the bank?
B: The bank is next to the bus station.
A: Excellent. And the mosque?
B: It is a little further on the left side, after the bridge.
A: Thank you again.
B: You are welcome. Enjoy your day!

A: Hello. Could you show me the way to the supermarket?
B: Sure. Go straight ahead, turn left at the traffic light, and the supermarket is on your right.
A: Left at the traffic light… okay. Is it far from the market?
B: No, it is right next to it.
A: Thank you.
B: Anytime. Do you want me to show the post office as well?
A: Yes, please.
B: Walk back to the main street and cross the square.
A: Cross the square… got it.
B: Turn right at the corner, and you will see the post office.
A: Perfect! You are very kind.
B: No problem. Good luck!

A: Excuse me, I am lost. How can I get to the church?
B: Walk straight ahead, then turn right at the second traffic light.
A: Straight ahead and right… okay.
B: The church is in front of the hospital.
A: Thank you very much.
B: You’re welcome. Take care!

A: Could you tell me where the square is?
B: Yes, it is near the bank. Walk straight, cross the bridge, and the square is on your left.
A: Cross the bridge and left… understood.
B: There are shops in front. You cannot miss it.
A: Thank you!
B: Have a nice day!

V. Exercises

Exercise 1: Translate into English

  1. Excusez-moi, où est la banque ?
  2. Tournez à gauche, puis traversez le pont.
  3. L’hôpital est en face de l’école.
  4. Je vais au marché chaque matin.
  5. La gare routière est à côté du commissariat.

Exercise 2: Complete the sentences with go / turn / cross

  1. ______ straight ahead.
  2. ______ the road carefully.
  3. ______ right at the supermarket.

Exercise 3: Role Play

In pairs, one student (A) asks for directions, and the other student (B) gives directions. Use the vocabulary and expressions from the lesson.

Conclusion

Asking for and giving directions is essential for moving around a town. With vocabulary for streets, buildings, and locations, learners can navigate and communicate effectively in English. Practicing dialogues helps beginners understand instructions and respond confidently.
