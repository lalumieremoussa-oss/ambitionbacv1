AT THE MARKET – AU MARCHÉ
I. Introduction

Aller au marché est une activité quotidienne très importante. En anglais, il est essentiel de connaître le vocabulaire des aliments, savoir demander le prix et comprendre les réponses du vendeur. Cette leçon aide les débutants à parler simplement au marché.

II. Vocabulary – Le vocabulaire du marché
1. Cereals (Céréales)
  • Rice – Riz
  • Corn – Maïs
  • Millet – Mil
2. Legumes (Légumineuses)
  • Bean – Haricot
  • Peas – Petits pois
  • Peanut – Arachide
  • Soy – Soja
3. Tubers (Tubercules)
  • Yam – Igname
  • Cassava – Manioc
  • Potato – Pomme de terre
  • Sweet potato – Patate douce
  • Taro – Taro
4. Vegetables (Légumes)
  • Okra – Gombo
  • Eggplant – Aubergine
  • Onion – Oignon
  • Garlic – Ail
  • Tomatoes – Tomates
  • Carrots – Carottes
  • Cabbage – Chou
5. Meat and Fish (Viandes et poissons)
  • Meat – Viande
  • Fish – Poisson
  • Shrimp – Crevette
  • Crab – Crabe
  • Liver – Foie
6. Fruits
  • Orange – Orange
  • Mango – Mangue
  • Banana – Banane
  • Pineapple – Ananas
  • Avocado – Avocat
  • Guava – Goyave
III. Asking for the price – Demander le prix

Pour demander le prix d’un produit au marché, on utilise les expressions suivantes :

AnglaisFrançais
How much is it?Combien ça coûte ?
How much are the tomatoes?Combien coûtent les tomates ?
What is the price of yam?Quel est le prix de l’igname ?
Astuce : on utilise is pour un nom singulier et are pour un nom pluriel.
IV. Giving the price – Donner le prix
AnglaisFrançais
It is 500 francs.Ça coûte 500 francs.
They are 1,000 francs.Ça coûte 1 000 francs.
The yam is 700 francs.L’igname coûte 700 francs.
V. Useful expressions – Expressions utiles
  • Expensive – Cher
  • Cheap – Pas cher
  • Too expensive – Trop cher
  • Money – Argent
  • Change – Monnaie
  • Insufficient – Insuffisant
VI. Reading – At the Market

Awa: Hello, mother.
Mother: Hello, Awa. How are you today?
Awa: I am fine, thank you.
Mother: Get ready, we are going to the market.
Awa: Great! I like going to the market with you.
Mother: Yes, we need food for the house.
Awa: Are we going to buy food for the popote?
Mother: Yes, we buy food for cooking today.
Awa: The market looks very busy this morning.
Mother: Many people come early to buy fresh food.
Awa: I can see many sellers and many buyers.
Mother: Yes, the market is always crowded on Saturdays.
Awa: Mother, what do we buy first?
Mother: We buy rice and tomatoes first.
Awa: I see tomatoes over there, they look nice.
Mother: Let us go and talk to that seller.
Seller: Good morning, madam. Welcome.
Mother: Good morning, sir.
Awa: Good morning, sir.
Seller: What do you want to buy today?
Mother: I want tomatoes for cooking.
Seller: These tomatoes are fresh and good.
Awa: They are red and beautiful.
Mother: How many kilos do you suggest?
Seller: Two kilos are good for the family.
Mother: Okay, I take two kilos.
Mother: How much are they?
Seller: They are 500 francs.
Mother: Oh no, that is too expensive.
Awa: Yes, mother, it is too much money.
Mother: Can you make it cheaper, please?
Seller: Madam, I can reduce the price a little.
Seller: I give you 400 francs.
Mother: That is better, but can you reduce more?
Seller: Okay, 350 francs, last price.
Mother: Thank you, that is cheaper now.
Awa: Mother, that is a good price.
Mother: Yes, we agree.
Seller: Here are your tomatoes.
Mother: Thank you very much.
Awa: Mother, let us buy fish for the popote now.
Mother: Yes, fish is good for today.
They go to the fish seller.
Fish seller: Hello, madam. Fresh fish here.
Mother: Hello. The fish looks good.
Awa: This fish is very big.
Fish seller: Yes, it is fresh and tasty.
Mother: How much is this fish?
Fish seller: It is 1,000 francs.
Mother: That is too expensive for us.
Awa: Mother, ask him to reduce the price.
Mother: Please, can you reduce the price?
Fish seller: Okay, I give it to you for 800 francs.
Mother: Can you make it 700 francs?
Fish seller: Hmm… okay, 700 francs.
Mother: Thank you, that is fair.
Awa: We are happy now.
Mother: Here is the money.
Fish seller: Thank you, madam.
Awa: Please, give us the change.
Fish seller: Here is your change.
Mother: Thank you very much.
Fish seller: You are welcome. Have a nice day.
Awa: Mother, are we finished shopping?
Mother: Yes, we buy everything for the house.
Awa: The market is noisy but interesting.
Mother: Yes, but now we go home.
Awa: I can carry the small bag.
Mother: Thank you, Awa, you are helpful.
Awa: I like helping you with the popote.
Mother: You are a good child.
They go home together happily.

Conclusion

Cette leçon permet aux débutants de parler facilement au marché. Grâce au vocabulaire, aux expressions et aux exercices, l’élève peut demander le prix, comprendre les réponses et communiquer simplement en anglais.
